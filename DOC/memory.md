## Product Direction

VANNIC is developing an industrial edge platform for commercial and industrial electrical infrastructure.

Core capabilities include:

- real-time electrical monitoring
- abnormal-condition detection
- event logging
- priority-based load management
- preventive control of approved non-critical loads
- edge intelligence
- cloud visibility
- analytics

VANNIC should not be presented as only an energy dashboard.

Preferred product story:

```text
Electrical Infrastructure
        ↓
VANNIC Industrial Edge Controller
        ↓
Local Intelligence & Control
        ↓
VANNIC GRiDMiND
```
